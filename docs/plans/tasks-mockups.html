Tasks mockups

Phase 0 of docs/plans/tasks.md. Built with Orbit's real tokens, type scale, buttons, tags and avatars. Names, companies and numbers are invented. Today is Thursday 12 November.

Static, apart from hover states. Underlined names are links: solid for contacts, companies and opportunities, dotted for teammates (they open a direct message in Chat).

01

Add task

  • One card for the Tasks page and the dialog from the bottom-right button. No header bar, no divider, one large title line with a one-line placeholder.
  • The chips are always there as quiet placeholders, so the card shows what it can do before you type. They fill in live as the text is understood: dates in blue, records in purple, an uncertain name in amber.
  • The card says who it will link before you save. A unique full name links. A first name with one match is shown as that person, so saving confirms it; a name with two matches asks. Tasks from agents, workflows and email never link on a first name.
  • Footer: the ClickUp list on the left, Add task with its Enter shortcut on the right.

03

Expanded rows

  • Enter, Space or a click opens a row in place. One open at a time; the list stays above and below. Title and description edit in place.
  • The contact strip carries the actions. The first action follows the kind of task worked out from the title (Call, Email or Invite); every action is always there.
  • The context line shows the opportunity's stage and value, the last activity and your own last email with the person.
  • Due, Remind me and the assignee are editable chips, with Tomorrow and Next week as quick dates. Open (Shift Enter) goes to the panel; Done (E) completes.
  • Prepare: a call brief or an email draft, made each morning for tasks due that day, or on demand. Only the person who prepared it sees it.

04

Done with an outcome

  • Done takes effect at once. On a linked task the row stays open for a short note and a next task; Skip, Esc or moving on just leaves it done.
  • The kind is preselected from the title. The note goes on the timeline of the linked person and company.
  • Orbit suggests the next task. You can change it, clear it or set its date. Save with Command Enter.

Choices to approve

  1. One add card everywhere: the Tasks page, the dialog and record pages. No header bar or divider, a 17px title line, six chips always visible, ClickUp list bottom left, Add task with ↵ bottom right. The purple send circle goes.
  2. Chip colours follow today's highlights: dates and reminders blue, records purple. An uncertain name turns amber and lists the choices inside the card, with "Don't link".
  3. A confirmation line under the chips ("Jane Cooper, Head of Operations at Northwind, is the only Jane") so you see the link before saving. In the card a first name with one match is proposed and saving confirms it; created anywhere else, a first name is never linked.
  4. Phone add is a bottom sheet with filled chips first and the rest scrolling sideways.
  5. Rows group under Overdue, Today, Upcoming, No date in All, with a one-line description preview on wider screens, and contact, company and opportunity chips.
  6. Names in titles are links: solid underline for records, dotted for teammates, which open a direct message in Chat.
  7. Unlinked tasks show a quiet "Link a person" chip in the row; the candidates appear when the row is expanded.
  8. The expanded row is a raised card inside the list, not a full-width band, with the contact strip first and the action that matches the kind of task as the one primary button.
  9. Call shows "Teams" beside it on desktop, since that's where it goes. Phones show just Call.
  10. The context line sits under the strip: opportunity, stage tag, value, last activity, your last email.
  11. Prepare lives inside the expanded row as a quiet card marked "only you see this". Without one, a Prepare button sits in the footer.
  12. A company-only task lists the company's people with numbers and a Link button, plus Call switchboard when the company has a number.
  13. Done with an outcome stays inline under the completed row: kind, note, a suggested next task with its date, Skip (Esc) and Save (⌘↵). The undo toast reads "Task done." with Undo and Z.
  14. The panel is non-modal and 440px wide on desktop, Ask Orbit in its header, Done on the title circle, no footer.
  15. Phone swipes use Capture's colours: green for Done, primary for Remind me, grey until armed.
  16. On a contact's page the expanded row drops the contact strip and puts the actions in one row.
  17. A keyboard strip at the foot of the Tasks page while a row is selected, plus "New" and "Changed" marks in the help for a while.
  18. New icons needed: phone, user, user circle, list (ClickUp), expand, undo, chevron up. Drawn here in the existing 14px, 1.4 stroke style.
