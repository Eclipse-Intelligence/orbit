Collaboration mockups

Phase 0 of docs/plans/collaboration.md. Built with Orbit's real tokens, type scale, buttons, avatars and icons. Names and companies are invented.

Dashed yellow labels are notes for review, not part of the interface. Use the Desktop and Phone switch on each screen. Hover messages, click reply counts, and type @ in screen 1.

01

The @ picker

  • The existing mention list (mention-input.tsx) with two group labels: People first, then Records. Same row style, same width cap (352px), same keyboard: arrows, Enter or Tab, Esc.
  • People show avatar, name and workspace role. Records keep today's "Kind · detail" line. You are left out of People.
  • Try it: the composer below is live. Type @, then a few letters.

    Companies

    Choices to approve or veto

    1. Chat in the sidebar sits right under Mail, with a grey count of unread conversations that turns purple when one mentions you.
    2. No presence dots (online or away) on people. The plan has no presence; only typing.
    3. Mentions of you get a primary wash and left bar on the whole message, and a stronger chip. Other people tags use the existing purple mention colour. Record links in sent messages are underlined in violet, as in Docs.
    4. Failed sends read "Couldn't send. Try again." with "Try again" as the button, to match the error rule. The plan says "Retry".
    5. The @ picker shows workspace role (Owner, Admin, Member) under each person, not a job title. It leaves you out.
    6. Threads view shows only room and DM threads, as cards with an inline Reply field and "2 new" counts.
    7. Room settings is a Sheet; new room is a dialog. Members can be removed by anyone in the room. Webhooks get Pause, Rotate secret and Hold to delete.
    8. Tasks: Mine, Tagged and (owners and admins) Everyone in the header. The hand-on menu says "Handing it on keeps you tagged". Tagged people get a read-only panel with a one-line notice.
    9. Doc discussion is a button in the doc bar with a count, and a right-hand panel that takes the backlinks rail's place while open.
    10. Shared email gets its own "Shared" tab after the split tabs, shown only while something is shared with you. Rows put the owner's avatar where the unread dot goes.
    11. Notes on phones sit below the thread, not in a sheet.
    12. The share button in Mark's reader turns into the people's avatars once shared. Shortcut Shift S.
    13. The teammate's compose card is titled "Mark's draft", and its locked states use one status line with a lock icon.
    14. Bell rows carry no content except a task title on "Done by John". Plain room messages never reach the bell or push.
    15. New icons needed: chat (speech bubble), threads, sliders (room settings). Drawn here in the existing 14px, 1.4 stroke style.
