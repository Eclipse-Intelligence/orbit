Time blocking mockups

Phase 0 of docs/plans/time-blocking.md. Built on the real Calendar page (week grid, 64px hours, 15 minute slots) with Orbit's tokens. Names, companies and meetings are invented. Today is Thursday 12 November, 11:20.

Static. Blue and teal cards are Outlook meetings from two calendars. Purple cards are task blocks. Dashed purple cards are Plan's proposals.

01

Calendar week view

  • The left menu has two sections. Calendars is collapsed by default (remembered per browser) and shows how many there are. Tasks lists open tasks you own or that are unassigned, grouped Overdue, Today, This week, Later, No date.
  • Each row: kind icon (call, email, meeting, other), title, linked person or company, and duration. Tasks already on the grid show their time and a purple kind icon; the rest are waiting to be placed.
  • Blocks are tinted purple cards with no colour bar, so they never read as meetings: done circle, title, then person and duration when there is room. A 15 minute block is one line.
  • Friday: four calls back to back, outlined as a run. Thursday 09:00: done earlier, greyed with a tick for the rest of the day. Tuesday: overdue, red with its mark, kept on its day. Thursday 14:30: placed by hand over Halden weekly, so it carries an amber edge and "Clashes with Halden weekly".
  • The calendar button in the toolbar still hides and shows the whole menu (\). Plan sits next to Day and Week.
02

Drag, resize and long-press

  • Dragging from the list: the row stays as a dashed placeholder, the card follows the pointer, and the slot it will land in shows solid, snapped to 15 minutes, with its day, time and duration.
  • Resizing: the bottom edge of a block grows a handle on hover. The label shows the new duration first, then the times. It snaps to 15 minutes; 5 and 10 come from the Duration chip.
  • Dropping a block on the list makes it date only. The drop zone opens at the top of the list and says what happens, including that the Outlook event goes.
  • iPad: hold a block to pick it up (the ring fills, then the card lifts). Dragging near the top or bottom edge scrolls the grid. Holding a row in the drawer closes the drawer and carries the task onto the grid.
  • Crops of the week, Wednesday to Friday.

03

Plan preview

  • Plan builds a preview for today and the next 14 days. Proposals show dashed on the grid; runs are outlined and labelled ("6 calls · 1h 30m"). Arrows move to next week, where the preview continues.
  • The side panel replaces the left menu while previewing. Placed is listed by day with runs called out. Each proposal can be removed here or dragged on the grid.
  • One line explains where learned times shaped it. Purple durations were suggested for tasks still at 15 minutes; durations you set are never changed.
  • Overdue tasks stay put unless you press Move (a slot today, or the next free day). Couldn't fit offers Next free day, Outside hours or Leave. Nothing is written until Apply.
  • Replan my blocks is off by default. On, it also moves blocks Plan placed earlier, to another time on the same day. Blocks you placed, like Thursday's Email Oliver Grant, stay.
04

Duration and Schedule in the task

  • Clicking a block opens the Tasks panel over the calendar. Duration sits under Due: 5, 10, 15, 30, 45, 60 or a custom number of minutes.
  • Schedule (S in the list) is the keyboard and phone route onto the calendar: the next free slot today at the task's duration, tomorrow morning, Monday morning, or a date and time. On a timed task it also offers Remove the time.
  • A clash shows as an amber line at the top of the panel.
  • Due shows a time only for timed tasks: "Today 14:30" and "Fri 13 Nov, 11:15" with a clock, "Today" and "Fri 13 Nov" without. A date-only task due today isn't overdue until tomorrow.
  • Remind me on a date-only task asks for a time first.

05

Phone

  • The agenda lists blocks in time order among meetings. Blocks keep the purple card and the done circle on the right, within thumb reach. Tap opens the task; the circle completes it with Undo.
  • The calendar button opens one drawer with Calendars (collapsed) and Tasks.
  • Tapping a task in the drawer, or Schedule in a task, opens the Schedule sheet. Duration is set in the same sheet.
  • No drag on phones, and no Plan: Plan needs the grid, so it starts at iPad width.

06

Settings › Calendars

  • Personal ("you, everywhere"). The main Outlook account says where blocks go.
  • Working hours per day, 09:00 to 17:30 Monday to Friday by default. Only Plan and Schedule read them. The Friday finish shows an edited day.
  • Learned times show one strip per kind: Monday to Friday by half hour, darker where you tend to do that work. The caption says what it learned from. Reset starts every kind from scratch.
  • Without write access to Outlook, the account says so and offers the reconnect (b).

07

Outlook, for reference

  • Blocks land in the main Outlook calendar as Busy and Private, with the task title, no attendees and no reminder (Orbit sends task reminders).
  • Colleagues and booking pages see Busy only. Moving one in Outlook moves the task; deleting it makes the task date only.

Choices to approve

  1. Blocks are purple, tinted and barless, with the done circle where meetings have their colour bar. Meetings keep their calendar colour. Purple is the existing violet accent, not a calendar colour, so a calendar can't clash with it.
  2. 15 minute blocks are one line (circle, title, duration) at the standard 64px hour. Person and duration move to a second line from 30 minutes.
  3. Done blocks grey out with a tick and strikethrough for the rest of the day, then go. Overdue blocks turn red with the Tasks page's red circle and an Overdue mark.
  4. Clashes use an amber left edge and the line "Clashes with {meeting}", and sit side by side with the meeting like any overlap.
  5. Runs are outlined with a count ("4 calls", "6 calls · 1h 30m") on the grid, both for Plan's runs and for back-to-back calls placed by hand.
  6. The left menu stays 240px. Calendars collapses to one line with a count; Tasks rows are two lines with the duration on the right. Timed tasks get a purple kind icon and their time.
  7. One toolbar button (calendar icon, \) still shows and hides the whole menu. On phones and iPad it opens one drawer with both sections.
  8. Plan's panel replaces the left menu while previewing, on the right, 372px. The grid keeps all seven days.
  9. Overdue in the preview shows the old and suggested time with Move and Leave each, and Move all on the group.
  10. Couldn't fit names the reason ("No 2 hour gap on Mon 16 Nov") and offers Next free day with the day it would use.
  11. Suggested durations are purple pills with a sparkle, in the panel and on the block, so they are visibly the AI's.
  12. Replan my blocks is a switch in the panel footer, off by default, with the rule spelt out under it.
  13. Drop on the list to remove the time, with a drop zone that says the day stays and the Outlook event goes. Schedule offers the same as Remove the time.
  14. Schedule is a menu beside Due on desktop (S on the Tasks page) and a bottom sheet on phones, with Duration in the same sheet.
  15. Due shows a clock only when timed. Date-only tasks read as today's day names; nothing claims 00:00.
  16. No Plan on phones. Phones get the agenda, Schedule and the drawer.
  17. Learned times are a 5 by 20 strip per kind (Monday to Friday, 08:00 to 18:00 by half hour) with one Reset for all kinds.
  18. The Outlook event body carries only "From Orbit" and a link to the task. No names beyond the title.
  19. New icon: a plain circle for "other" tasks, in the existing 14px, 1.4 stroke style.
